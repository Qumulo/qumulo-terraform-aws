## NOTE:
Place pre-run or post-run files here.  The hook for pre/post run files points to this directory.

## WARNING:
You may also place entire user-data override files here.  Customizing user-data comes with significant risk.  We recommend you collaborate with your Qumulo SE if you need to customize user-data for an instance deployed by the Qumulo Provider.  In most cases pre-run or post-run additions will provide the necessary customization.
